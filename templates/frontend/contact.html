Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our organization and work

Optimizing People is a Christian non-profit organization dedicated to making a difference through Christ's love. We provide medical care, water supply, food distribution, and spiritual support to impoverished communities in rural areas.

You can get involved by volunteering, donating, or spreading awareness about our work. Fill out the contact form above or reach out to us directly to learn about current opportunities.

We are based in California, United States, and currently focus on serving communities in Nigeria. We plan to expand to other parts of the world as the Lord leads us.
