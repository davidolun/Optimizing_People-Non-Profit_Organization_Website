Who We Are

A Christian non-profit organization dedicated to making a difference through Christ's love

Our Story

Our Foundation

Here at Optimizing People, we understand that sometimes, all it takes to change the world is a little support. Since the founding vision received, we have been determined to make an impact in Christ's strength.

Optimizing People is a dream and kingdom vision that began with revelation about upbringing in the remote part of Nigeria. It is inspired further by the Holy Spirit on ministering to society through provision of water outlets, basic human needs, free medical/mental health care to the impoverished community in rural areas.

Our Mission

It is an international, non-denominational, and evangelical non-profit organization. This indicates that we are Christian-based believers; upholding the word of God and the leading of the Holy Spirit.

Our operation is situated in California, United States, from where we are reaching out to Nigeria and in future to the other parts of the world as the Lord leads.

It is the heart cry of our Lord Jesus Christ that everyone is saved! Therefore, our desire is to walk in this path; proclaiming the gospel. We participate in evangelical crusades but we are not a church or a fellowship. Our services are equally opened to Christians and non-Christians.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Love

We believe that God is love and we must live in love, showing compassion to all people regardless of their background or circumstances.

Service

We are committed to serving others with humility and dedication, following the example of Jesus Christ in our daily work.

Faith

Our work is grounded in faith and trust in God's plan, believing in His good plans for all believers despite life challenges.

Global Impact

We are committed to reaching communities worldwide, starting from Nigeria and expanding as the Lord leads us.

Inclusivity

Our services are open to both Christians and non-Christians, welcoming all who need help and support.

Sustainability

We focus on creating lasting change through sustainable solutions that empower communities for long-term success.
