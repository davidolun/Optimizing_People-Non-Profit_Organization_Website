About US

Here at Optimizing People, we understand that sometimes, all it takes to change the world is a little support. Since the founding vision received, we have been determined to make an impact in Christ's strength.

Optimizing People is a dream and kingdom vision that began with a passion about upbringing in the remote part of Nigeria. It is inspired further by the Holy Spirit on ministering to society through provision of water outlets, basic human needs, free medical/mental health care to the impoverished community in rural areas.

It is an international, non-denominational, and evangelical non-profit organization. This indicates that we are Christian-based believers, upholding the word of God and the leading of the Holy Spirit. Our operation is situated in California, United States, from where we are reaching out to Nigeria and in future to the other parts of the world as the Lord leads.

It is the heart cry of our Lord Jesus Christ that everyone is saved! Therefore, our desire is to walk in this path, proclaiming the gospel. We participate in evangelical crusades, but we are not a church or a fellowship. Our services are equally opened to Christians and nonchristians.

At Optimizing People, we are to offer evangelical messages, materials, and spiritual enlightenment including basic needs and health care support towards promoting life. We believe in the Lord Jesus Christ and reach out in His love towards mankind. It is our delight if you can please join us by supporting our efforts in making a measurable difference in the lives of others.
