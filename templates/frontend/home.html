About Us

Optimizing People is an international, non-denominational nonprofit organization dedicated to transforming lives through faith-inspired humanitarian work. Founded on a divine vision in 2020, we extend Christ's love by providing Bibles, healthcare, and essential support to underserved communities.

Based in California, we currently serve communities across Nigeria and are expanding globally as God leads. Our work is rooted in biblical values, compassion, and service to all—regardless of faith or background.
